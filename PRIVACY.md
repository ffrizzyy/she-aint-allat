# Privacy policy for she aint allat

Effective 16 October 2026. Applies to version 0.1.0 and later until replaced.

## The short version

Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The extension makes no network requests of its own. We cannot see your lists, your pop-ups, your letters or your browsing, because we never receive them.

## What the extension stores

All of this is kept in your browser's extension storage on your own device. It is not sent anywhere and it does not use browser sync.

- Your triggers: the words, names, handles, places or link patterns you add, with their settings.
- Your pop-ups: the lines and pictures you add. Pictures are shrunk on your device and stripped of camera data (EXIF) when added.
- Your reasons it ended, if you write any.
- Your unsent letters, if you write any.
- An event log: the time, the website domain, the level, and what you did (for example "went back"). It never contains page addresses, search text or page content. Entries older than 12 months are dropped.
- Settings: language, region for support numbers, a snooze time, and the address of your own page if you set one.
- Your app lock, if you turn it on: a random salt and a one-way hash of your passphrase. The passphrase itself is never stored.
- One short note for tomorrow, if you write one.
- Short-lived data for the current browser session only: recent trigger counts, an unlock timer and similar. It is cleared when the browser closes.

## What the extension reads

Only on sites you allow, one at a time. On those sites it reads the page address, the page title, search boxes you type into, visible text, image descriptions and image file names, and links. It compares them with your triggers in memory. It does not save them and it does not send them anywhere. It ignores password, payment and one-time-code fields.

## Permissions, in plain words

- **Storage and unlimited storage:** to keep your lists and pictures on your device.
- **Scripting:** to start watching a site after you allow it.
- **Web navigation:** to notice when a page changes without reloading.
- **Site access:** asked for one site at a time, only when you press "Allow this site". You can remove a site at any time.

## What can leave your device

Nothing leaves automatically. These things happen only when you press a button:

- Opening a support website, or a phone number, from the Support page.
- Opening WhatsApp or your messaging app with exactly the message you wrote on the Support page. That message then goes through those services under their own policies.
- Copying that message to your clipboard.
- Saving an export file or a CSV to your computer. You decide where it goes.

## Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell or rent your data, because we do not have it.

## Deleting your data

Settings has a "Delete everything" button that erases all stored data and removes every site you allowed. Removing the extension from your browser also deletes its data. If you forget your app lock passphrase, the lock screen has an option that erases everything and starts over.

## Children

This extension is not directed at children.

## Not medical care

This is a self-help tool. It is not therapy or medical care. If you are in danger or thinking about hurting yourself, contact your local emergency services. The Support page lists options by region.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with a new date.
