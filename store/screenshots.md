# Screenshots and images

I could not take these for you, because they need a real browser. Here is exactly what to capture.

## Rules
- Chrome: 1 to 5 screenshots, 1280x800 (or 640x400), PNG or JPEG, no transparency.
- Firefox: any clear size, 3 to 5 is plenty.
- Use made-up data only. Never show a real name, a real person's photo or your private pictures. Use sample words such as "Alex" and "banana".
- Keep text readable at small size. Light mode for the first shot, dark mode for one more.

## The five shots
1. **The pop-up.** Open `demo/index.html`, set Level 1, type "Alex" in the search box. Capture the blurred page with the card on top.
2. **Settings, triggers.** The side menu plus the Your triggers card with three sample triggers showing their small tags.
3. **Your pop-ups.** The pop-ups card with two lines and one pleasant, neutral picture (a landscape or a pet).
4. **Insights.** Use the extension for a few days, or add a handful of sample events, then capture the per-day table and the hour-by-weekday table.
5. **Support or Night mode.** The Support page with a region chosen, or the Night mode page.

## How to capture exactly 1280x800
1. Open the page, press F12, then Ctrl+Shift+M (Cmd+Shift+M on Mac) for the device toolbar.
2. Choose "Responsive" and type 1280 x 800.
3. Open the DevTools command menu with Ctrl+Shift+P, type "screenshot", and choose "Capture screenshot".
4. In Firefox use the camera icon in the responsive design tool (Ctrl+Shift+M).
